# Score Quest music

Background music and sounds streamed by Tristan's Score Quest (a private family app).

- `star-hopper.mp3`, `atomic-fury.mp3`, `golden-death.mp3`, `mother-of-chaos.mp3`, `earthshaker.mp3`, `event-horizon.mp3`, `hold-the-fjord.mp3`, `green-rain.mp3`, `hold-back-the-flood.mp3` (Flood Guard), `level-up-lounge.mp3` (dashboard menu), `pop-explorers.mp3` (Pop Explorers), `brass-key.mp3` (Case Files): themes made with Suno.
- `thunder-crack.mp3`: "Thunder crack 2B" by Jud McCranie, from Wikimedia Commons
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Thunder_crack_2B.ogg), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Converted to MP3 by Wikimedia; the game plays short
  sections of it at varied pitch.
- `duel-sfx.mp3`: one file holding many short sound effects, trimmed, mixed to mono and normalized from these
  CC0 (public domain dedication) or public-domain recordings:
  - "20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)" by StarNinjas, OpenGameArt (CC0)
  - "RPG Sound Pack" by artisticdude, OpenGameArt (CC0)
  - "Impact Sounds", "RPG Audio" and "Sci-fi Sounds" by Kenney, kenney.nl (CC0)
  - "80 CC0 creature SFX #2" by rubberduck, OpenGameArt (CC0)
  - "CC0 Deep Monster Roar" by trazzz123, OpenGameArt (CC0)
  - "Large Wings Flap" by AntumDeluge, OpenGameArt (CC0)
  - "Ice Breaking/Shattering" by IgnasD, OpenGameArt (CC0)
  - "Fire Staff Sound Effects" by LEGIT Audio, OpenGameArt (CC0)
  - "Explosion-LS100155" (Fg2) and "Explosion 10" (tcpp), Wikimedia Commons (public domain)

- `flood-sfx.mp3`: Flood Guard sounds (rushing water loops, splashes, wood and rock impacts), trimmed, mixed to mono and
  normalized from these CC0 (public domain dedication) recordings:
  - "40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX" by rubberduck, OpenGameArt (CC0), https://opengameart.org/node/86789
  - "Impact Sounds" by Kenney, kenney.nl (CC0)
